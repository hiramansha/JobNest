Frontend Developer

TechNova

About the Role

We are looking for a talented and motivated Frontend Developer to join our growing team. You will work on modern web applications and create clean, responsive, and user-friendly digital experiences.

This role is a great opportunity for someone who enjoys solving problems, working with modern technologies, and collaborating with a creative team.

Responsibilities

  • Build responsive and user-friendly web interfaces.
  • Work closely with designers and backend developers.
  • Write clean, maintainable, and reusable code.
  • Test and improve website performance and usability.
  • Participate in team discussions and development activities.

Requirements

  • Good understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • Familiarity with responsive web design.
  • Basic understanding of Git and GitHub.
  • Strong problem-solving and communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

What We Offer

Competitive salary
Flexible working environment
Learning opportunities
Friendly team culture
Career growth opportunities
Professional development
